# Rank commitment trial — 2026-11-13

Status: isolated, unadopted prototype. Installed support10/D10 baseline unchanged.
User aims for supported territory and thick shogi, with low consolidation and opposite-flank counterplay as an option when support is unsustainable.

## Implementation

`prepare.py` copies the adopted source snapshot. Only the experimental snapshot adds `src/rank_commitment.h` and an evaluation hook; `evaluation.patch` records the difference. `RAKUYOU_RANK_COMMITMENT_CP` is tested at 0/10 (absent: 0). No root move bonus or unsupported-pawn reward.

A side is flagged once if an unpromoted fifth-rank pawn on files 2–8 has a directly supporting unpromoted gold/silver on relative rank 6, is directly attacked by an enemy rook/dragon/bishop/horse, and enemy geometric participants outnumber its own. Participants include direct controls and one rear slider layer exposed when each direct participant vacates; duplicates count once. This ignores pins, hands, timing and legal exchanges and does not prove sustainability.

The flagged side loses at most 10 cp before the existing `(1-progress)` taper. Both colors use the same rule. The hook shares the support diagnostic lane, so that field includes the experimental correction, independently of the baseline support coefficient. There is no explicit reward for a solid king camp or waiting for enemy commitment.

## Interpretation and next step

The small correction can change the line toward relinquishing an overmatched rank, while the two controls remain stable. It does not yet express the user's requested sequence of consolidating low, waiting for the enemy's commitment, then counterattacking. Target common-depth score does not establish improvement, and six searches cannot establish strength or broad safety.

Keep this trial unadopted and the installed baseline unchanged. Review the concrete target ON line before changing the design: immediate P2e counterplay may itself be premature. Do not increase the coefficient to force G5b. Any subsequent experiment should distinguish safe consolidation and viable counterplay from merely abandoning supported territory. Long matches remain user-run. All artifacts here are in ignored `results/`; only the resume map is tracked by this task.
